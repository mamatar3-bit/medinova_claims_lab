# Denial Reason Codes Guide

Each denied claim carries one MediNova denial reason code. Use this guide to explain the denial and the next step.

## DN01 – Prior authorization required but not obtained
Ask the provider to request retro-authorization or file an appeal with medical records. Usually resolved by: Provider.

## DN02 – Out-of-network provider not covered by plan
HMO plans cover out-of-network care only for emergencies. Member may appeal if no in-network provider was available. Usually resolved by: Member.

## DN03 – Timely filing limit exceeded
Claim was received after the filing deadline. Provider may appeal only with proof of timely submission. Usually resolved by: Provider.

## DN04 – Duplicate claim
Same service was already processed. No action needed unless the service was performed twice. Usually resolved by: Provider.

## DN05 – Service not covered under plan
Service is excluded in the plan's benefits summary. Member may appeal with a letter of medical necessity. Usually resolved by: Member.

## DN06 – Additional information or medical records needed
Provider must send the requested records within 45 days or the claim stays denied. Usually resolved by: Provider.

## DN07 – Member not eligible on date of service
Coverage was not active. Verify eligibility dates and resubmit if coverage was active. Usually resolved by: Member.

## DN08 – Specialist referral required (HMO)
HMO members need a PCP referral before seeing a specialist. Provider can submit the referral and resubmit. Usually resolved by: Provider.
